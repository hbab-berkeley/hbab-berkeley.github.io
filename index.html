About

Our story.

Healthcare is one of the biggest industries in the world, and most students never see how it actually runs. We started HBAB to change that.

Who we are

The business side of health, for every major.

The Healthcare Business Association at Berkeley is a student organization for undergraduates who want to understand how healthcare and the life sciences work as industries: how drugs get developed and funded, how hospitals are managed, how health tech companies grow, and where business skills fit in.

Our members come from across campus. Some are pre-med students who want to understand the system they'll work in. Others are aiming for biotech, consulting, venture, or operations. What we share is curiosity about the space and a commitment to helping each other get there.

What we do

Consulting for the business of health.

HBAB consultants combine backgrounds in biology, public health, economics, business, and engineering. Each project is staffed by a dedicated student team that works directly with your organization from first conversation to final recommendation.

We work with organizations at every stage, from early-stage startups testing an idea to established companies exploring a new market.

Biotech & PharmaDigital HealthMedical DevicesHealth Systems & ProvidersNonprofits & Public HealthEarly-Stage Startups

Services

How we can help.

Every engagement is tailored to your goals. Most projects draw on one or more of these areas.

Market research & sizing

Size and segment your market, understand demand, and identify the opportunities worth pursuing.

Competitive landscape

Map competitors, benchmark offerings, and find where your organization can stand apart.

Go-to-market strategy

Define target customers, positioning, and channels, and build a plan to reach them.

Product & commercialization

Evaluate new products, technologies, or indications and chart a realistic path to market.

Operations & process

Analyze workflows and operations to reduce costs and improve the patient or customer experience.

Financial analysis

Build models and business cases that support pricing, investment, and growth decisions.

Our approach

From question to recommendation.

A clear, structured process keeps every project focused on what matters to your organization.

01

Scope

We meet with your team to define the problem, the key questions, and what a successful outcome looks like.

02

Research

We gather the evidence: industry and market data, competitor analysis, and interviews with experts and customers.

03

Analyze

We turn findings into insight with structured frameworks and models, checking in with your team along the way.

04

Deliver

We present clear, actionable recommendations in a final presentation and report your team can put to work.
